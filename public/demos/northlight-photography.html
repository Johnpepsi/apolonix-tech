Hello, we are Alex and Sam.

We have photographed weddings and families for ten years, and we still get nervous before every first frame. That is probably why people tell us they forgot we were there.

We shoot in natural light, direct only when you want direction, and edit every photograph by hand. You get a sneak peek within 48 hours and the full gallery within weeks, not months.

Common questions

When will I get my photos?

A sneak peek of 10 to 20 images arrives within 48 hours. Full wedding galleries are ready in 6 weeks. Portrait and family galleries are ready in 2 weeks.

How do I book a date?

A signed agreement and a 30% retainer hold your date. The balance is due 2 weeks before a wedding, and on the day for portrait and family sessions.

Do you travel?

Yes. Sessions within 50 km of the studio have no travel fee. Beyond that, travel is $0.60 per kilometre, and we can quote overnight travel for destination weddings.

Can I print the photos?

Every gallery includes a print release, so you can print anywhere. You can also order prints and albums straight from your gallery.

What happens if it rains?

We reschedule portrait and family sessions at no cost. For weddings, we plan covered backup spots with you during the planning call.
